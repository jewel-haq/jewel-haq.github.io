I study how CO2 and reservoir fluids move and deform porous rock — coupling flow and geomechanical simulation to assess caprock integrity under cyclic injection, and building low-cost instrumentation where commercial sensors are impractical. My tools span continuum-scale reservoir simulation (CMG GEM/STARS, ANSYS Fluent), molecular dynamics (LAMMPS), and custom optical sensing hardware. The throughline is subsurface CO2 storage: understanding it well enough to store it safely at scale.
