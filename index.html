I study how CO2 and reservoir fluids move through — and deform — porous rock, coupling flow and geomechanical simulation to assess caprock integrity under cyclic injection. Where instrumentation doesn't exist off the shelf, I build it. The throughline across a mechanical-engineering base, a biomedical-sensing detour, and a petroleum-engineering present is subsurface CO2 storage: understanding it precisely enough to store it safely, at scale.
