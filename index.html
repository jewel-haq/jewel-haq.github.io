01 About

I am an M.Sc.Eng. candidate and Assistant Experimental Engineer at the Petrophysical & Analytical Lab, Department of Petroleum & Mineral Resources Engineering (PMRE), BUET. My work spans experimental petrophysics and computational reservoir–geomechanics simulation, with a growing focus on geological CO2 storage and caprock integrity — the technical core of Carbon Capture, Utilization & Storage (CCUS).

My academic path is deliberately multidisciplinary: mechanical engineering (B.Sc.), biomedical physics and instrumentation (M.S.), and now petroleum & subsurface engineering (M.Sc.Eng.). This combination gives me a working fluency across experimental instrumentation, continuum-scale simulation (CMG GEM/STARS, ANSYS Fluent), and molecular-scale modeling (LAMMPS) — tools I bring together to study how energy and fluids move through porous and engineered systems.

Next goal: a Ph.D. focused on CO2 storage and utilization (CCUS) — coupled flow–geomechanics of storage reservoirs, caprock/seal integrity, injection-induced stress paths, and the molecular-to-reservoir-scale behavior of CO2 in subsurface formations.

02 Research Focus

Current and target research areas, centered on subsurface CO2 storage.

CO2 Storage & Caprock Geomechanics (CCUS)

Coupled flow–geomechanics simulation of cyclic CO2 injection in depleted gas reservoirs, with emphasis on caprock integrity, geomechanical risk, and injection strategy — using CMG GEM. This is my primary line of research and the direction I intend to carry into doctoral study.

Target Ph.D. area

Reservoir & Geomechanics Simulation

Coupled flow–geomechanics modeling of reservoir rock behavior under injection/production cycles using CMG GEM & STARS.

CFD & Heat–Mass Transfer

Computational fluid dynamics and transport phenomena in energy and drying systems using ANSYS Fluent, COMSOL and SimScale.

Molecular Dynamics of Energy Systems

Molecular-scale simulation (LAMMPS, OVITO, VMD) to complement continuum-scale reservoir and transport models.

Instrumentation & Sensor Development

Design and sensitivity optimization of low-cost optical sensors, applied to biomedical pressure sensing and petrophysical measurement.
